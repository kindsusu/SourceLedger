# Cross-site collection audit

Completion is evaluated separately for job termination, product discovery, price and option extraction, and each value's verification state. If the end of a catalog cannot be evidenced, coverage remains `unknown`.

## Shared checks

- Record when product links, JSON-LD arrays, option lists, or output bounds are truncated.
- Reconcile each displayed price and condition against the final capture and SQLite observation.
- Track load-more controls, filters, pagination, and hidden cards as separate coverage areas.
- Keep site-specific path recognition consistent between discovery and extraction.
- Preserve evidence for errors, blocks, and incomplete pages rather than counting them as success.

## Verification

Do not use a historical site's offer count or runtime as a fixed public acceptance target. Synthetic fixtures exercise term prices, options, truncation, missing links, and partial coverage. Approved live research records remain in ignored local workspaces.
